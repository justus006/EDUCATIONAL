ScholarHub Academic Honor Code

Global Standards for Ethical Peer Learning

1. Strictly Educational & Research Purpose: ScholarHub exists solely to connect students worldwide for academic study, research feedback, and conceptual problem-solving.

2. Guided Mentorship Over Direct Copying: When assisting with assignments, explain underlying principles, formulas, debugging steps, and citations so peers genuinely learn.

3. Proper Attribution: Always cite published papers, datasets, RFCs, or textbooks when sharing literature reviews or empirical findings.

4. Respectful Global Discourse: Engage constructively with students from all universities, regions, and academic backgrounds.
